Privacy

On your device

Your recovery words and signing keys stay in the extension. Recovery words are stored encrypted with your local password. Optional node credentials are also encrypted locally. Account addresses, connection choice, outgoing Activity and hashes of completed site requests are kept in local extension storage. Chrome storage sync is not used.

Network requests

In Explorer mode, explorer.blockfab.org receives the account addresses, transaction queries, asset metadata and image requests needed to show your wallet. flush.markets receives swap quote requests. Locally signed transactions are sent to the selected network's broadcast endpoint. These services can observe your IP address and request data. Confirmed transactions are public.

When you connect the site, flush.markets receives your approved public address, public key and network. Each transaction request still requires separate review in the wallet. Recovery words and your unlock password are never sent.

In own-node mode, requests go to your paired RPC nodes. They receive the necessary addresses, RPC credentials and signed transactions. Remote nodes require HTTPS; local loopback HTTP is supported. Returning to Explorer deletes saved node credentials and revokes optional node permissions.

Analytics and control

The extension implements no analytics or advertising and does not sell your data. It does not create a remote wallet account or use remote signing. External Explorer, site and node operators handle the requests described above under their own service practices.

Use Chrome's extension controls to remove the wallet and its local storage. Keep your recovery words privately if you need to restore it. Removing local data does not remove public blockchain transactions or information already received by network services.

Support

Questions and corrections can be filed in the repository issues. Never include recovery words, private keys, passwords or node credentials.
